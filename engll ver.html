Who I am

About

My name's Kam. I translate video games from English into French, driven by one simple belief: a good game deserves a French version that never reads like a translation. No stiff, mechanical phrasing, no meaning lost along the way — just dialogue that lands, descriptions players actually want to read, and narration that respects the original tone while feeling fully at home in French.

I work on early access titles just as happily as on finished games needing a full pass, solo or alongside other translators on larger volumes. Glossary building, consistent proper nouns and game mechanics, and localized wordplay — everything is built so a French player forgets they're not playing in the game's original language.

The process

How I work

1

Send me a sample

A text file, a dialogue sheet, or even just a demo build — I look at the volume, the tone, and any technical constraints (character limits, variables).

2

Quote & glossary

I send you a per-word rate and a timeline. For longer projects, I build a glossary of proper nouns and recurring terms before starting.

3

Translation in batches

I deliver in sections rather than one final block, so you can sign off on the tone from the first lines and course-correct early if needed.

4

Proofreading & integration

A final proofreading pass, then light support if tweaks are needed once the text is live in the game.

What people ask

FAQ

Do you work on early access projects?

Yes, happily. I can translate as updates roll out, keeping a living glossary so terminology stays consistent from version to version.

How long does 50,000 words take?

Usually 3 to 4 weeks for a polished result, longer if the text is dialogue-heavy or the project needs several rounds of proofreading.

Do you invoice in USD?

Yes — payment through PayPal or Wise works well for US studios, no conversion hassle on your end.

Do you proofread existing translations too?

Yes — send me the source text and the current French version, and I'll give you detailed feedback before proposing a full edit if needed.
